## Summary

Openly-licensed, plain-language guides on handling and storing staple crops after harvest — reducing
food loss for smallholder farmers. Each guide summarizes established good practice from authoritative
open sources (FAO and similar), written for non-specialists and translators.

## Scope

In scope: concise how-to guides for common crops and storage situations (grain drying and storage,
root/tuber storage, tomato and leafy-vegetable handling, pest-safe storage basics). Out of scope:
crop-specific chemical/pesticide dosing, anything requiring regulated agronomic advice, or region-specific
legal guidance.

## Risk

Low. These summarize well-established, published post-harvest practice from authoritative bodies; they
give general educational guidance, not prescriptive advice for a specific farm, and involve no personal
data. Each guide must cite its sources and stay within established practice.

## Approach

Each task produces one self-contained Markdown guide with a clear structure (why it matters, steps,
common mistakes, sources), a CC-BY header, and citations to the open source material it summarizes.

## Definition of shipped

Guides published openly (CC-BY), source-cited, and handed to at least one farmer-support organization
for use in training.
